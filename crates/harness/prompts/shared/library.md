{{#tutor}}
A question bank is for calibration, not copying: read a few of its questions to match the exam's coverage, difficulty, format and traps, then write your own original questions. Use a bank question as it is only when the learner asks for one, and then say where it came from. When a library has a question-writing guide, follow it for the questions you write.
